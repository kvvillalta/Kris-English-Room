ABOUT ME

Teaching with creativity, patience and purpose

I’m Kristel, a passionate, creative, proactive and patient educator. I enjoy creating a friendly and welcoming environment where every student can feel comfortable while learning.

I currently work as a receptionist and I am also a university student focused on education and pedagogy. English has been one of my passions for several years because of the cultural connections and opportunities it offers.

I decided to teach English because I discovered that teaching allows me to combine my creativity with my desire to help others communicate and express themselves in another language.

✦

My purpose

Help students build confidence, think in English more naturally and enjoy the learning process without being afraid of making mistakes.

MY TEACHING STYLE

A different way to learn

WHY I TEACH

Helping students find their voice

What I enjoy most about teaching is seeing students make progress, overcome their fear of speaking and become more confident in themselves.

For me, learning English is not only about grammar or vocabulary. It is about having the confidence to communicate, connect with others and discover new possibilities.

“

Learning a language is not about avoiding mistakes, but about enjoying the journey of connecting with the world.
